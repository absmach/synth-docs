# `synth-layout` Crate

`synth-layout` computes schematic placement and wire routes. One layouter is shared by the KiCad exporter, the MCP tools, and the browser preview, so the views agree.

## Output

A `Layout` containing:

- `ComponentPlacement` — position, rotation, and mirroring on the sheet.
- `WirePath` — wire segments between pins.
- `PowerFlag`, `PowerFlagKind` — power symbols and their orientation.
- `NetLabel`, `LabelSpot`, `HierarchicalLabel` — net labels and their anchors.
- `GroupBox`, `TextAnnotation`, `TextKind` — group boxes and notes.
- `Rotation`, `SheetSize`, `PinSide` — sheet and symbol geometry.

## Modules

`maxrects` (rectangle packing), `placer`, `route`, `netclass`, `score`, `ops`, `footprint_resolve`, `kicad_footprint_loader`, `kicad_lib_loader`, `kicad_zip`, and `qualify_facts`.

## Sidecar

The optional `<design>.schematic.layout.toml` holds **sheet millimetres**. `schematic_sidecar_path` resolves the canonical filename. This is a different coordinate space from the PCB placement sidecar; passing one where the other is expected silently misplaces parts.
