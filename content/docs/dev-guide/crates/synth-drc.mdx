# `synth-drc` Crate

The `synth-drc` crate validates physical board layouts against manufacturer design rules (minimum trace width, pad-to-pad clearance, drill-to-track distance, annular ring sizes).

## Diagnostic Pipeline (`synth-diagnostics`)

DRC violations are categorized by severity (`Error`, `Warning`, `Info`) and emitted with exact spatial coordinates and net identifiers for easy debugging.
